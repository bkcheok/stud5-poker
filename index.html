How stud works

  • Everyone antes 10. You get one hole card (only you can see it) and one card face-up.
  • Three more face-up cards follow — 3rd, 4th and 5th street — with a betting round after each.
  • The strongest showing hand acts first each street. Best five-card hand at showdown takes the pot.
  • In rooms, the host's device deals and keeps score; friends connect directly, peer-to-peer.
